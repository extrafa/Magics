# Magic Tricks Support

Magic Tricks is a toolkit of mentalism tricks for iPhone. Your phone passes you
secret signals through vibrations, so you can reveal what a spectator chose.

Need help? [Email support](mailto:usmoder@gmail.com?subject=Magic%20Tricks%20Support).
Please tell us your iPhone model and iOS version, and what happened.

## Frequently asked questions

### My phone doesn't vibrate during a trick

The secret signal is a vibration, so it only works if vibrations are on. On your
iPhone, open Settings, then Accessibility, then Touch, and make sure Vibration is
on. Then open Settings in Magic Tricks, go to "Vibration Settings" and tap "Try
Vibration" to check it.

### How do I practice the signals?

Open Settings in the app and tap "Practice Signals". You can also change speed,
strength and grouping of the vibrations in "Vibration Settings".

### How do I close a trick?

Press and hold the area at the top left of the screen. A tip shows you where it
is when you open a trick. You can turn the tip off with the "Show Exit Tip"
setting.

### Phantom Draw won't connect

Phantom Draw needs two devices that are close to each other, with the trick open
on both. On the device you give to the spectator, tap "Give to spectator". On the
other one, tap "This phone is mine". The first time, iOS asks to allow Local
Network access. If you said no, turn it on in Settings, then Privacy & Security,
then Local Network, then Magic Tricks.

### How do I restore my purchase?

Magic Tricks has a one-time purchase that unlocks the extra tricks. To get it back
on a new phone or after reinstalling, open Settings in the app and tap "Restore
Purchases". You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

### What happens to my photos?

Photos you add to Magic Gallery stay inside the app on your device. The app has no
way to upload them anywhere. See the privacy policy for details.
